# 5-minute demo script

Start with **Reset demo** (top right), viewing as **WBL Coordinator**. You're Dana Johnson, an English teacher who was just handed the WBL coordinator job.

0. **School profile** (`/intake`, or the "Start here" card). Enter ZIP **80226** (Denver Metro). Mark Healthcare and Arts & Media as "A lot". Click **Use sample catalogue** to see courses mapped to industries, then look at the local job market. On **Your priorities**, show the growth-vs-readiness chart and save 3 focus industries.
1. **Journey home** (`/journey`). Your focus industries now show at the top. "New to this? Start here." Point out the two parts and the Learn / Do / Grab pattern.
2. **B1 Why WBL matters.** Pick 2–3 outcomes, click **Draft my why**, then **Save as our why**. This text appears in outreach later.
3. **B2 to B4** (skim). Show the employer empathy map (switch to "Auto shop"), the self-check score, and the clickable lifecycle (each stage shows how many of *your* employers are in it).
4. **S1 Map your network.** (The *Local industry explorer* tab is filtered to your focus industries.)
   - *Who do you know?* Type `Front Range Auto` as the business. A duplicate warning appears: **Mr. Patel already has a relationship**. Click **Join as helper**.
   - *Student-sourced* tab: show the QR code, then **Claim** Diego's food-truck lead.
5. **S2 Reach out.** Pick *Bear Creek Animal Hospital*, then switch between Email, Text and Call script. Note your "why" is included and a personal invite link is generated. Click **I sent it**. It moves to *Invited*.
6. **The employer's view.** Switch *Viewing as* to **Employer** (opens Sol Bakery's link). On a phone-sized window: "Hi Maria, Ms. Johnson invited you…" → pick **Career fair table** → **Continue** → **I'm in!** → confirmation.
7. Switch back to **Coordinator**. Open **Pipeline** and show Sol Bakery's timeline ("signed up through their invite link").
8. **S3 Handle objections.** Pick "We don't have time." Show the smaller ask, then click through the role-play.
9. **S4 First engagements.** Answer the 3 questions, then **Plan it** for a career fair and include Sol Bakery. Click **Mark as happened**: the employers move to **Level 2 · School visit**.
10. **Today.** A "Thank Maria at Sol Bakery" nudge appears. Click it to open **S5**, add a student thank-you note, then open *Their page* to see the note on the employer's side.
11. **S6 Create opportunities.** Choose *Front Range Auto Repair* and **Host students on site (Level 3)**. Show the Colorado paperwork checklist with sources. Click **They said yes!**
12. **Dashboard.** The headline "employers who moved up a level this semester" goes up.
13. Switch to **District viewer**. Totals across 3 schools, with no employer names.
