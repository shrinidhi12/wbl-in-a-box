# Staff network brainstorm (20 minutes)

**Instructions:** Spend 5 minutes on each prompt. Write a business and a person whenever you can.

1. **Where do you spend money every week?** (coffee, groceries, gym, mechanic, salon)
2. **Who are your people?** (spouse/partner, siblings, neighbors, friends: where do they work?)
3. **Who have you taught or coached** who now works locally or owns a business?
4. **What groups are you part of?** (faith community, clubs, sports leagues, volunteer orgs)
