# First steps menu: Levels 1 and 2

## Level 1 · Conversation
| Activity | Time | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| ☕ Informational conversation with a student | 20–30 min | Phone, video or coffee | Anyone; a great very first ask |
| 📚 Curriculum advice for a teacher | 30 min | Virtual | Managers and experts who want to shape what students learn |

**Prep students first:** research the business, 3 questions, school email with a teacher copied, a thank-you within 2 days.

## Level 2 · School visit
| Activity | Time | Where | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🎪 Career fair table | 2–3 hrs, once | School | First-timers; bundles many employers |
| 🎤 Classroom presentation | 1 hr | School or virtual | Anyone with a story to tell |
| 🤝 Mock interviews | Half a morning | School | Employers comfortable with teens; HR staff |

**Tip:** Every Level 1–2 activity should end with a thank-you and a natural "would you ever host a student?" (Level 3).
