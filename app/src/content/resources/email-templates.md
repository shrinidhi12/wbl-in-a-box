# Outreach templates
*Personalize every message. Short beats polished.*

## 1. You know them personally
**Subject:** A quick favor for Lincoln High students?

Hi [First name],
It's [your name], [how they know you]. I'm helping Lincoln High connect students with local businesses, and I immediately thought of you.

Would you be up for [small ask, e.g. an hour at our career fair on Oct 15]? Here's a short link with details. It takes 2 minutes: [invite link]

No pressure at all. Happy to answer questions anytime at [phone].
[Your name]
