# Common employer concerns, and what's really behind them

| What you hear | What it usually means | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| "We're too busy." | I can't lose staff hours right now. | A shorter, virtual or off-season ask |
| "What about liability?" | I don't know what I'm on the hook for. | Explain what the school covers; start on campus |
| "Teens aren't reliable." | I've had a bad experience. | Prepared students, a teacher present, a clear agenda |
| "We don't have anything for them to do." | I don't know what a good experience looks like. | A ready-made format: tour, talk, mock interviews |
| "Talk to HR." | I'm interested but not sure I'm allowed. | A one-pager they can forward; offer to call HR together |
| "We tried this before." | The school dropped the ball. | One named contact, and follow-through you can prove |
| "We're too small." | I assume you want big companies. | Small businesses are often the best first partners |

**Remember:** a "no" to an internship is rarely a "no" to everything.
