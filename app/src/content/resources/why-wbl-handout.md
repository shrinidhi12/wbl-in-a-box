# Why work-based learning?
*A one-page brief for staff and school leaders*

## What it is
Experiences where students learn from real employers: guest speakers, career fairs, tours, job shadows, projects, mentoring and internships. Colorado calls this the **work-based learning continuum**: learning *about*, *through* and *at* work.

## Why it matters for our students
- **Career clarity.** Students see real jobs, not just job titles.
- **Skills.** Communication, reliability and problem solving in real settings.
- **Networks.** Every student meets adults who can vouch for them, not only those with family connections.
- **Motivation.** Classes feel relevant when students see where they lead.

## Why it matters for our community
- Local employers meet their future workforce.
- Young people see a future close to home.
