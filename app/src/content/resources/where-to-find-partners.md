# Where to find employer partners

## Start inside (warmest)
- **Staff**: their dentists, mechanics, relatives, former students, side jobs
- **Students**: their own jobs, and their parents' and relatives' workplaces
- **Families**: add a "Where do you work? Would you help?" question to back-to-school forms
- **Alumni**: especially local business owners
- **Booster clubs, PTA, sponsors**: they already support you

## Then widen
- Local **chamber of commerce** and economic development council
- **Workforce centers** (Colorado Workforce Centers) and regional workforce boards
- **Industry associations**: builders, healthcare, manufacturing
- **Intermediaries**: CareerWise Colorado, community college partnerships
- **Public employers**: city, county, fire, water, transportation, libraries
- **Neighborhood businesses** within walking distance of school

## Colorado in-demand industries
Healthcare · Construction & skilled trades · Advanced manufacturing · IT & software · Energy · Financial services
