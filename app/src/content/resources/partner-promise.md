# Our promise to every employer partner

**[School name]** will:

1. **Give you one contact.** [Name], [phone], [email]. Always the same person.
2. **Respect your time.** Clear dates, start and end on time, no surprises.
3. **Prepare our students.** They'll have researched you and come with questions.
4. **Handle the logistics.** Rooms, permission slips, transport and paperwork.
5. **Say thank you within 48 hours,** with notes from students.
6. **Show you the impact.** What students learned and what they did next.
7. **Recognize you publicly,** with your permission.
8. **Never pressure you.** You choose how much to do, and "not now" is always OK.

*Signed,* ____________________  *WBL Coordinator*
