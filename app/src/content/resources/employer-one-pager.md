# Partner with Lincoln High: help students see their future

**Students at Lincoln High want to meet people like you.** Many don't have family connections in professional careers. An hour of your time can open a door.

## Ways to help (start small!)
- ☕ **A conversation**: 20 minutes with a curious student, or advice for a teacher on what to teach
- 🎪 **Career fair table**: 2–3 hours, once
- 🎤 **Classroom presentation**: 1 hour
- 💻 **Virtual classroom visit**: 30 minutes from your desk
- 🤝 **Mock interviews**: half a morning

*Later, if you enjoy it:* host or mentor a student, offer a project or micro-internship, and eventually a formal internship or apprenticeship.

## What we handle
Scheduling, student preparation, permission slips, and paperwork. You'll always have one contact.

## What you get
- The chance to give back to your community
- Recognition as a **Lincoln High Community Partner**
- Thank-you notes from students you've inspired
- Over time, a pipeline of local young talent
