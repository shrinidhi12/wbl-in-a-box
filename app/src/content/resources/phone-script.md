# Phone call script

**Open (warm):** "Hi [name], it's [your name] from Lincoln High. [How you're connected.] Do you have two minutes?"

**Why:** "We're trying to make sure every student gets to meet real people in real careers, not just the kids with family connections."

**The ask (small, specific):** "Would you be up for [one hour at our career fair on Oct 15]?"

**Listen.** If they hesitate: "What would make that easier?" (See the Objection playbook.)

**If yes:** "Wonderful! I'll text you a link with the details. It takes two minutes. I'll be your contact the whole way."

**If not now:** "Totally understand. Would it be OK if I checked back in [month]?" Note the date.

**Close:** "Thank you, really. Our students will love this."
