# Guest speaker run of show (55 minutes)

**Before:** Send the speaker the date, room, parking, check-in, and a 3-line description of the class. Students prepare 3 questions each.

| Minutes | What |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Teacher introduces the speaker |
| 5–20 | "My path": how they got here, including the detours |
| 20–30 | "A day in my job": tools, people, problems |
| 30–50 | Student Q&A |
| 50–55 | Thank-you and how to learn more |

**Suggested prompts for speakers:** What did you want to be at 16? What's the hardest part of your job? What would you tell your teenage self?

**After:** Student thank-you notes within 48 hours.
