# Student prep sheet: meeting an employer

## Before
- **Research:** What does this business do? Who are their customers?
- **Write 3 questions.** Good starters:
  - How did you get into this work?
  - What does a typical day look like?
  - What skills matter most here?
  - What do you wish you'd known at my age?
- **Dress:** Clean, neat, closed-toe shoes for worksites.

## During
- Introduce yourself with a handshake (or wave) and your name.
- Phone away. Listen, take notes.
- Ask at least one question.

## After
- Write a thank-you note within 2 days.
- Reflect: one thing that surprised me, one thing I want to learn more about.

**Safety:** All communication with employers goes through your teacher or school email.
