# Touchpoint calendar (per partner, per year)

| When | Touchpoint | Effort |
|---|---|---|
| Within 48 hrs of any activity | Thank-you + student notes | 10 min |
| 2–3 weeks later | Share impact: a photo, quote or outcome | 5 min |
| Monthly (active partners) | Quick check-in: "anything new at the shop?" | 2 min |
| Each semester | Invite to the next level or a repeat activity | 15 min |
| Winter break | Holiday card signed by students | 15 min |
| End of year | Recognition: newsletter feature or certificate | 20 min |

**Rule of thumb:** no partner should go more than 30 days without hearing from you during the school year.
