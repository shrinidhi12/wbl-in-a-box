# The school–employer relationship lifecycle

**Discover → Connect → First engagement → Deepen → Opportunity → Sustain & champion**

## The five levels of employer engagement
| Level | Name | What the employer does | Colorado continuum | Paperwork |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conversation | A student reaches out for an informational conversation, or a teacher consults them on curriculum | Learning ABOUT work | None (communication norms) |
| 2 | School visit | Career fair, classroom presentation | Learning ABOUT work | Campus visitor check-in |
| 3 | Host & mentor | Hosts a student on site, job shadow, ongoing mentor or buddy | About → Through | Permission slips, site visit form, mentor agreement |
| 4 | Project | Provides a project or micro-internship | Learning THROUGH work | Project agreement; pay rules if paid |
| 5 | Formal placement | Formal internship, WBL placement, apprenticeship | Learning AT work | Training agreement, insurance, youth employment rules |

**Our rule:** never ask for Level 5 first. Earn each step with a great experience.
