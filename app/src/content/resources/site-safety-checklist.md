# Worksite safety checklist
> ⚠️ **Sample only. Have your district review.**

- [ ] Named supervisor for students at all times
- [ ] Safety orientation planned for arrival
- [ ] Required safety gear available (eyewear, gloves, hard hats, etc.)
- [ ] Areas students may **not** enter are identified
- [ ] Tasks reviewed against hazardous-occupation limits for minors (Colorado CYEOA; federal FLSA)
- [ ] Emergency exits and first-aid location shown to students
- [ ] Emergency contact list from school on hand
- [ ] Group visits only; no one-on-one isolation with a student
- [ ] Photo/media rules agreed with school
