When an employer hesitates, **listen first**. Repeat back what you heard: "So the main worry is time during your busy season?"

Most objections fall into three buckets:

- **Time:** "We're too busy." → Offer something shorter, virtual, or in a slower season.
- **Risk:** "What about liability?" → Explain what the school handles and start with on-campus activities.
- **Uncertainty:** "I don't know what I'd do." → Give them a simple format, agenda and prepared students.

The move is almost always the same: **shrink the ask** until it's an easy yes, then deliver a great experience. A "no" to an internship is rarely a "no" to everything.
