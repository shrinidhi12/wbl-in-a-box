**Signs an employer is ready for more:** they ask "what's next?", they offer ideas, their staff want to be involved, or they mention hiring needs.

**How to propose the next level:**

1. **Make it specific.** "Would you host 4 students for a half-day job shadow on Nov 12?" beats "would you do more?"
2. **Bring a draft.** A one-page description they can edit is far easier than a blank page.
3. **Say who does what.** The school handles recruiting students, permission slips and transport. They provide a supervisor and a safe place.
4. **Be upfront about paperwork,** and do as much of it as you can for them.

**Paperwork grows with the level.** Hosting a student (Level 3) needs permission slips and a site visit form. A paid micro-internship (Level 4) brings in pay and hours rules. A formal placement (Level 5) needs a training agreement, insurance and a check against Colorado youth employment rules. The checklist below shows exactly what's needed.
