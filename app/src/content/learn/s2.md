**Warm beats cold.** If someone you know can make the introduction, use it. If you're reaching out directly, say how you're connected ("my student's mom works with you").

A great first message is:

- **Personal.** Written by you, from you, not "the WBL program".
- **Short.** Five sentences or fewer.
- **Specific.** One clear, small ask with a date: "an hour at our career fair on Oct 15".
- **Grounded in your why.** One sentence on why it matters for your students.
- **Easy to answer.** A link they can click, or "just reply yes".

**Follow up within a week**, and try a different channel. If you emailed, call or stop by. Silence usually means "busy", not "no".
