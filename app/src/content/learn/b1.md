Work-based learning (WBL) is any experience where students learn from real employers, from a guest speaker to a paid internship. Colorado describes it as a continuum: **learning about work**, **learning through work** and **learning at work**.

**For students**, it answers "what could I actually do?" It builds the skills employers say they want, like showing up, communicating and solving problems. It also builds something many students don't have: **a professional network**. Most jobs are found through people you know, so students without family connections start behind. WBL closes that gap.

**For your school**, WBL makes classes feel relevant, supports students' career plans (ICAP in Colorado), and builds community goodwill.

**For your community**, local businesses meet the people who could be their future workforce, and young people see a future close to home.
