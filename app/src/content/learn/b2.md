Employers usually *want* to help young people. The barriers are practical:

- **Time and capacity.** Small businesses run lean. An afternoon away is a real cost.
- **Liability and safety.** "What if a student gets hurt?" "Do I need special insurance?"
- **Working with minors.** Uncertainty about rules for hours, tasks and supervision.
- **Paperwork.** Fear of forms, background checks and agreements they don't understand.
- **Unclear benefit.** "What's in it for us?", especially before they've seen students in action.
- **Past bad experiences.** A no-show student or a school that never followed up.
- **Not knowing who to talk to.** Schools are hard to navigate from the outside.

Notice that **most of these are about the size or clarity of the ask**, not the idea itself. That's good news: you control the ask.
