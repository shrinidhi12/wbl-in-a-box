Every strong partnership moves through the same stages:

1. **Discover.** You identify a business and someone who knows them.
2. **Connect.** A warm, personal first contact.
3. **First engagement (Levels 1–2).** A **conversation** (a student's informational interview, or a teacher asking for curriculum advice), then a **school visit** like a career fair or classroom presentation.
4. **Deepen (Levels 3–4).** They **host and mentor**: tours, job shadows, an ongoing mentor or buddy. Then they offer a **project** or micro-internship.
5. **Opportunity (Level 5).** A **formal placement**: internship, WBL placement or apprenticeship.
6. **Sustain and champion.** They come back each year and introduce you to other employers.

| Level | Name | What the employer does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Conversation | Talks with a student or advises a teacher |
| 2 | School visit | Career fair, classroom presentation |
| 3 | Host & mentor | Hosts a student on site, job shadow, ongoing mentor/buddy |
| 4 | Project | Provides a project or micro-internship |
| 5 | Formal placement | Internship, WBL placement, apprenticeship |

Relationships don't always move in a straight line. "Not now" is a normal stage, not a failure. Note when to check back and keep the door open.
