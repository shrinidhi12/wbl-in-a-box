**Start with who you already know.** Warm connections convert far better than cold outreach.

- **Staff.** Every adult in your building has a dentist, a mechanic, a cousin in IT, a former student who owns a business.
- **Students and families.** Students know where their parents, aunts and neighbors work, and where they work themselves.
- **Alumni.** Graduates who stayed local are often eager to give back.
- **Booster clubs, PTA and sponsors.** These businesses already support your school.

**Then widen the circle:**

- Your local **chamber of commerce** and economic development office
- **Workforce centers** and industry associations
- **Intermediaries** such as CareerWise Colorado and regional workforce boards
- Colorado's **in-demand industries**: healthcare, construction, advanced manufacturing, IT and energy. Check the CWDC Talent Pipeline Report for your region.

Aim for **one named person** at each business, not just a company name.
