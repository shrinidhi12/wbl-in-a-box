**Keep the relationship warm between engagements:**

- **Thank within 48 hours.** A personal note plus student thank-yous.
- **Share impact.** A photo, a quote, "three students signed up for the CNA class".
- **Touch base every few weeks.** It doesn't always need to be an ask.
- **Recognize publicly.** Newsletter, social posts, a Community Partner window sign.

**When you introduce students to employers:**

- **Prepare students:** research the business, three good questions, what to wear, how to follow up.
- **Prepare the employer:** who's coming, what students are learning, and what a great visit looks like.
- **Keep it safe:** communication goes through school channels, groups not one-on-one, a staff member is reachable.
- **Debrief:** collect reflections and turn them into thank-yous.
