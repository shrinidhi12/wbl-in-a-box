**Start with Levels 1 and 2.** They're low risk for everyone, need almost no paperwork, and give you a reason to say thank you and ask again.

**Level 1: Conversation.** It's the smallest possible yes, and it's often where relationships really start.
- A student (well prepared) asks for a 20-minute **informational conversation** about the employer's career.
- A teacher asks for **curriculum advice**: "What should my students be able to do by the time they apply to you?"

**Level 2: School visit.** The employer comes to you.
- **Career fair table.** Great for first-timers, and one event gives you a dozen engagements in an afternoon.
- **Classroom presentation**, in person or virtual.
- **Mock interviews** for employers comfortable with teens.

**Match the ask to the employer:**
- **Short on time?** A conversation or a virtual classroom visit.
- **New to working with teens?** A career fair table, with a teacher in the room.
- **Already comfortable with teens?** Mock interviews, then invite them to host (Level 3).
