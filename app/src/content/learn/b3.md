Winning an employer is about the **experience you give them**, not the pitch.

1. **One consistent person.** They should always know exactly who to call.
2. **Small first asks.** Start with an hour, not a semester.
3. **Make it easy.** Parking info, a clear agenda, students who are prepared.
4. **Respect their time.** Start and end on time. Never make them chase you.
5. **Say thank you fast.** Within 48 hours, ideally with student notes.
6. **Show impact.** What did students learn? What did they decide to do next?
7. **Recognize them publicly.** Newsletter, social media, a "Community Partner" sign.
8. **Invite the next step.** Partners who are never asked again assume they aren't needed.

Keeping a partner costs far less than finding a new one. Most strong programs grow by deepening a few relationships, not by mass outreach.
